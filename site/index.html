Lineup

Starts every healthy player with a game, then the best projections for the slots left. Projections adjust each game for the opponent's defence and shot suppression, home ice, and back-to-backs.

Reading the grades

Matchup grade compares the opponent with an average NHL team: for skaters, goals and shots they allow; for goalies, win chance and how many goals the opponent scores. B2B means the team also played yesterday.

Value is the projected contribution to your weighted categories in this one game: each category's gap to an average fantasy starter, in standard deviations, summed. Above 0 beats an average starter. Change the weights on the Categories tab.

Week plan

Outlined cells are the recommended starts for that day. Footer rows count players with a game against your slots: red means an empty slot you could fill with a pickup, amber means a player with a game will sit.

Category strength

Category weights

Raise a category you are chasing this week, lower one you have won or are punting. Weights change the lineup picks.

Specialists by category

Top NHL skaters per game in the chosen category who are not on your roster. By default it hides the 140 highest-value skaters, since a 10-team league rosters about that many. It cannot see other fantasy rosters, so check availability in Yahoo.

My roster

Status Out and IR keep a player out of every recommendation; DTD players can start but rank lower. Active mirrors what you have set in Yahoo, so the Lineup tab can list the changes to make.

Your edits are saved in this browser. To use the same roster on another device, export here and replace site/roster.json in the repository.
